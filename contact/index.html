The quickest way to reach a plumber is to call. The line is answered 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including holidays. We are a call-only service, so there is no form to fill out and no waiting for an email reply when water is on the floor.

If your problem is not urgent, call anyway and book a same-day appointment when one is available.

What to have ready when you call

Four things that speed up the call

  • 1Your address Include the nearest cross street if you are in a larger building.
  • 2What you see or hear Water where it should not be, a smell, a sound, a fixture that quit.
  • 3What you already shut off Main water valve, water heater power or gas, a fixture shutoff.
  • 4When it started A leak that began this morning is different from one that has run for a week.

If it is an emergency

If water is spraying or flooding, shut off the main water valve first, then call. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility and 911 from outside before you call a plumber. Nicor Gas serves Elmwood Park, but check your bill if you are unsure of your gas provider.

Keep people away from wet outlets, appliances and the electrical panel until the water is stopped.

Our office and how to get here

We are at 7508 W Grand Avenue in Elmwood Park, on the Grand Avenue corridor. Grand Avenue is a state (IDOT) street, so allow extra time at rush hour. The Elmwood Park Metra station is also on Grand Avenue.

Most work happens at your property, not at our office, so please call before visiting.

What happens after you call

A person answers, asks for your address and listens to what you see. If water is running, they tell you what to shut off while a licensed plumber is lined up. You hear the price and the plan before work starts, so nothing is decided after the fact.

If the work needs a permit or an inspection, we handle that with the Village building department. Inspections are booked two working days ahead, so a planned job such as a water heater swap or a sewer repair is scheduled around that notice.

Calling about a rental or a shared building

About a third of Elmwood Park households rent, and many buildings are two-flats or small apartment buildings. If you rent, tell your owner or property manager about the problem as well, since they decide who pays and who may enter the unit.

In a shared building, tell us which unit you are in and whether neighbors have the same problem. A backed-up stack or a failed main shutoff affects more than one apartment, and the answer to that question changes what the plumber brings.

Other village offices you may need

The Village of Elmwood Park building department at 11 West Conti Parkway handles plumbing permits and inspections. Village Hall and the public library are also on Conti Parkway.

Report street flooding and public sewer problems to the Village. Report a gas smell to Nicor Gas, and any immediate danger to 911.
