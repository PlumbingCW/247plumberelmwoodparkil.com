Plumbing Service Area: Elmwood Park and Nearby Villages

We serve Elmwood Park and the villages around it. Emergency calls are answered 24/7.

  • Elmwood Park ZIP 60707
  • River Grove ZIP 60171
  • 24/7 emergency line
24/7 emergency line (773) 207-0518

Elmwood Park sits about nine miles northwest of the Loop in Cook County. The village borders the City of Chicago to the north and east, River Forest to the south and River Grove to the west. The closest villages are a short drive apart.

Below is how we group the towns we serve. River Grove has its own page because it is our nearest neighbor and has its own plumbing permit office, ZIP code and flood history.

Chicago neighborhoods and other places we get asked about

Customers also call from the Chicago neighborhoods of Galewood and Montclare, which sit east of the village line, and from Harwood Heights, Norridge and Forest Park. Call the 24/7 line to confirm that your address is in our area.

We do not build a separate page for every town, because plumbing rules, water sources and sewer types differ by village and we only write about the ones we can describe accurately.

How we decide which village gets which page

A town page only makes sense when we can say something true and specific about it. For River Grove we can name the Des Plaines River flood history, the Grand Avenue and River Road crossings and the village's own building department. For the other villages on this page we serve homes and businesses but we do not describe local rules we have not checked.

If you live in one of those villages, the plumbing work is the same as in Elmwood Park: water heaters, drains, sewer lines, leaks and fixtures. The difference is the permit office, so we confirm with your village before work that needs one.

What stays the same wherever we work

Every call gets a clear price before work starts and a plumber. Emergency calls get a same-day appointment when one is available. Water in the area comes from Lake Michigan through the City of Chicago for Elmwood Park and River Grove alike.

Local permit rules differ, so we check with the village where the work happens. See our River Grove page for the details that apply west of Elmwood Park.
