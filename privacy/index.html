Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026. This policy explains what information we handle when you call us or use this website.

24/7 emergency line (773) 207-0518

Elmwood Park 24/7 Plumbing operates this website and a telephone service line. This policy describes what we collect and why.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your address and a description of the problem so we can schedule service.

We use this information to answer your request, to schedule and perform work and to improve our service.

This website

This site has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site working.

The map on our pages is provided by Google Maps. Loading it may share technical data with Google under Google's own privacy terms.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the people who perform your service, with service providers who help us operate, and where the law requires it.

We keep call and scheduling records only as long as needed for the purposes above and for legal and accounting needs.

Your choices

You may ask what information we hold about you or ask us to delete it, subject to legal requirements. Contact us using the details on our contact page.

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the current version.

Questions about this policy?

Call us or use the details on our contact page.
