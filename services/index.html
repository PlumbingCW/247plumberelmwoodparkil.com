Plumbing problems rarely announce which trade they belong to. A wet ceiling can come from a pipe, a drain or a water heater. This page groups our plumbing services by what is going wrong so you can find the right place to start in Elmwood Park, Illinois.

If you are not sure which page fits, call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. A plumber will tell you where to begin, and anything urgent gets handled first.

Hot water and heating

No hot water is one of the most common reasons people call us. Tank heaters wear out, burners and elements fail, and boilers in older homes need their pressure and pumps checked before each winter.

Water Heater Repair

Leaking tanks, no hot water, pilot and ignition faults, bad thermostats and worn valves on gas, electric and hybrid heaters, with after-hours help when a tank lets go.

Water Heater Replacement

Failed or flooded tanks swapped for new gas, electric or hybrid units, with venting, valves and expansion control done to code and the old heater taken away.

Water Heater Installation

Planned water heater installs and moves: sizing, fuel choice, venting, drain pans, expansion tanks and the details that keep a new unit safe for years.

Tankless Water Heater

Tankless water heater installs, repairs, error code diagnosis and descaling flushes, plus honest advice on gas supply, venting and whether on-demand heat suits your home.

Boiler Repair

Boiler leaks, pressure trouble, circulator pumps, air in the lines, valves and expansion tanks, with honest guidance on burner and gas problems that need another trade.

Drains, sewers and flood control

Elmwood Park is served by combined sewers, so basement drains and sump pumps matter more here than in many suburbs. These pages cover everything from a slow kitchen sink to a collapsed sewer lateral.

Drain Cleaning

Clogged sinks, tubs, washer lines and floor drains cleared with a drain machine, plus a camera look when a clog keeps coming back.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water jetting for recurring clogs from grease, scale and roots, with a camera look first so the pipe is right for it.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Main sewer line backups cleared at the cleanout with a cable and camera, plus advice on what a backup means in a combined-sewer village.

Sewer Line Repair

Cracked, rooted or collapsed sewer pipe confirmed by camera, then repaired by spot repair, trenchless lining or replacement, with honest trade-offs.

Sewer Inspection

A sewer camera inspection shows roots, cracks, sags and buildup inside your line, so decisions about buying, cleaning or repair rest on facts.

Sump Pump Repair and Installation

Pump stopped, pit rising, or no backup at all? We repair and replace sump pumps, add battery or water-powered backup, and explain backwater valves for sewer-side flooding.

Basement Waterproofing

Interior drain tile with a sump, sealed pipe penetrations, window well drains and backflow protection. We handle the plumbing side and tell you plainly when the problem is structural.

Leaks, burst pipes and supply lines

Most homes in the village were built before 1970, so supply lines and service lines are a regular part of our work. Detection comes before digging.

Leak Detection

A climbing water bill, a warm floor, a stain that keeps returning. We find hidden leaks in slabs, walls, ceilings and buried lines with sound, heat and pressure testing.

Burst Pipe Repair

A split pipe, a failed fitting or a frozen line gone wrong. We stop the damage, cut out the failure and repair copper, galvanized, PEX, PVC and cast iron lines.

Water Line Repair

The pipe between the street and your meter carries all your water. We find the failure, handle the 811 locate and repair or replace the line, with lead line rules explained.

Repiping

Galvanized, aging copper or polybutylene lines that keep leaking. We replace supply piping in occupied homes with PEX or copper, whole house or in phases.

Fixtures, installs and commercial work

Toilets, faucets, disposals and new installations, plus plumbing for shops, restaurants and multi-unit buildings.

Toilet Repair

Running, leaking, clogged or cracked toilets repaired, plus new toilet installation with the old one removed. Licensed plumbers answer the 24/7 line.

Faucet Repair

Dripping, spraying or stuck faucets, tub valves, under-sink shutoffs and outdoor spigots repaired or replaced by licensed plumbers, day or night.

Garbage Disposal

Jammed, humming, leaking or dead disposals repaired or replaced, with the dishwasher drain and sink connections handled by licensed plumbers.

Plumbing Installation

New sinks, tubs, showers, laundry hookups, appliance water lines and bathroom plumbing roughed in and connected by licensed plumbers.

Commercial Plumbing

Floor drains, restrooms, leaks and backups at restaurants, shops, offices and multi-unit buildings, with after-hours repairs from licensed plumbers.

Water Softener and Filtration

Water softener, whole-house filter and reverse osmosis installation and service for Elmwood Park homes, with honest advice on whether you need one.

How to choose between a repair and a replacement

A repair is usually the right answer when one part has failed and the rest of the system is sound. Replacement makes sense when a fixture or appliance is at the end of its life, or when repeated repairs cost more than the part they protect.

Our plumbers explain both options and the price of each before work starts, so you make the call with the facts in front of you.

Planned work versus emergencies

Emergency pages on this site, such as burst pipe repair and sewer line cleaning, say what to do while you wait. Planned work, including water heater installation and repiping, goes best when scheduled ahead because the Village building department handles permits and inspections.

The Village of Elmwood Park follows the Illinois State Plumbing Code and asks contractors to show a State plumbing licence, insurance and a bond. Permit rules vary by job, so ask the building department what yours needs.
